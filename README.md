
# Healthcare Operations Intelligence Ireland

## Project Overview

This project explores Ireland's long-term care system through publicly available data, focusing on Home Care, Nursing Homes and Unpaid Family Carers.

The objective is to understand how care services are distributed, how demand is changing and what challenges affect people receiving and providing care.

Government policy, funding and regulation will be considered as a cross-cutting dimension.

## Research Question

How does Ireland's long-term care system respond to the growing demand for care through home support, nursing homes and unpaid family caregiving?

## Analysis Areas

1. **Home Care Operations** — home support hours, people receiving support, waiting lists and geographic distribution.
2. **Nursing Home Intelligence** — registered nursing homes, bed capacity and geographic distribution.
3. **Unpaid Family Carers** — number, demographic profile and unpaid care responsibilities.
4. **Long-term Care Demand** — ageing population and demographic indicators related to care demand.
5. **Caregiver Health & Wellbeing** — available indicators on the health and wellbeing of paid home care workers and unpaid family carers.

Government policy, funding and regulation will provide context across the five areas.
