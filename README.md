# Healthcare Operations Intelligence Ireland

## Project Overview

This project explores Ireland's long-term care system using publicly available data, with a focus on Home Care, Nursing Homes and Unpaid Family Carers.

The objective is to understand how care services are provided, how the demand for care is changing and what challenges may affect people receiving and providing care.

Government policy, funding and regulation are considered cross-cutting dimensions throughout the project.

## Research Question

**How does Ireland's long-term care system respond to the growing demand for care through home support, nursing homes and unpaid family caregiving?**

## Analysis Areas

1. **Home Care Operations** — Home Support hours delivered, waiting lists, people receiving support and geographic distribution, depending on data availability.
2. **Nursing Home Intelligence** — Registered nursing homes, bed capacity and geographic distribution.
3. **Unpaid Family Carers** — Number of unpaid carers, demographic profiles and caregiving responsibilities.
4. **Long-term Care Demand** — Population ageing and demographic indicators related to potential care needs.
5. **Caregiver Health & Wellbeing** — Available indicators on the physical and mental health and wellbeing of paid home care workers and unpaid family carers.

Government policy, funding and regulation will provide context across all five areas.

## Methodology

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow:

1. Business understanding
2. Data collection and source evaluation
3. Data cleaning and preparation
4. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
5. Descriptive and statistical analysis, where appropriate
6. Data visualization
7. Interpretation of findings and recommendations

## Data Sources

The project prioritizes official and publicly available data from Irish institutions and European statistical sources, including:

- **CSO** — Central Statistics Office
- **HSE** — Health Service Executive
- **HIQA** — Health Information and Quality Authority
- **NTPF** — National Treatment Purchase Fund
- **Department of Health**
- **Eurostat** — European statistical data
